About

Prachurya studies Computer Science & Engineering at SRM Institute of Science and Technology. Outside coursework, the practice stays consistent: pick a real, specific problem, then build the full product around it — interface, application logic, persistence, and deployment.

Persistence that fits the product

Study Quest keeps local development simple and uses shared database storage when hosted. PageTurn combines IndexedDB caching with account sync for reading across devices.

Useful fallback paths

When a service is unavailable, the product should still guide the user. Study Quest can create a balanced schedule from saved subjects and study preferences without an external generation service.

End-to-end ownership

Every project here runs the full distance — interface, persistence, integrations, and deployment — rather than stopping at a demo.
